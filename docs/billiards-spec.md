# STEAL THE MOON — Saturn Billiards converged plan (5-agent roundtable, 2026-09-19)

Governing rule (unanimous): "THE EXPLOSION IS NEVER THE EVENT. THE ORBIT AFTER THE
EXPLOSION IS THE EVENT." No object ever receives free momentum. Nuclear events are
discrete impulses (the lever); the Long Torch is continuous Δv (the winch); Mimas is
never "pushed" by a bomb — it is levered loose through engineered momentum transfers.
Real JPL Horizons ephemeris for every date. "Real Saturn. Fictional crime."
